This paper proposes Fast3Dcache, a training-free geometry-aware caching framework that accelerates 3D diffusion inference while preserving geometric fidelity. The method introduces a Predictive Caching Scheduler Constraint (PCSC) to dynamically determine cache quotas and a Spatiotemporal Stability Criterion (SSC) to select stable features for reuse. Comprehensive experiments show that Fast3Dcache achieves up to a 27.12% speed-up and a 54.8% reduction in FLOPs, with minimal degradation in geometric quality.
