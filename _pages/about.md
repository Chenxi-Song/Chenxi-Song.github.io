<div class="home__intro">
<p>Hi, I'm <b>Chenxi Song</b>, currently a postdoctoral researcher at AGI Lab, Westlake University (Jan 2025 &ndash; Jan 2027), supervised by Prof. <a href="https://icoz69.github.io/">Chi Zhang</a>. I received my Ph.D. degree in Engineering from Jilin University in 2024, where I focused on 3D Computer Vision and Computer Graphics under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://dce.jlu.edu.cn/info/1066/7660.htm">Shigang Wang</a>, with co-supervision from Prof. <a href="https://wei-jian.github.io/about/">Jian Wei</a> and Prof. <a href="https://dce.jlu.edu.cn/info/1181/5220.htm">Yan Zhao</a>.</p>

<p>My current research interests lie in <b>world models</b>, <b>video generation models</b>, and <b>3D &amp; 4D scene reconstruction and generation</b>, with the hope of advancing world models for world understanding, real-world simulation, and the game, film and television industries. I am actively engaged in the academic community, serving as a reviewer for top-tier AI conferences and journals including NeurIPS, CVPR, ECCV, AAAI, MM, and SIGGRAPH. I am honored that our earlier world model work, <b><a href="https://worldforge-agi.github.io">WorldForge</a></b>, was selected as a CVPR 2026 Highlight. More recently, we released <b><a href="https://chenxi-song.github.io/worldinworld/">World in World</a></b>, a 4D world model that explores the real world inside a video. I will continue to focus on controllable video generation and world model research. Welcome to collaborate and exchange ideas!</p>
</div>
